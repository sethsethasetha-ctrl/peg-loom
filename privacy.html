Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Pegloom does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Pegloom handles information when you use the game on an iPhone or iPad.

Information We Collect

Pegloom does not require an account and does not collect personal information, device identifiers, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or other user content.

Local Game Data

Pegloom stores game progress, unlocked challenges, best ratings, sound preferences, and haptic preferences locally on your device using Apple's standard local storage. This information is used only to provide game functionality. It is not sent to the developer or to third parties. The operating system may include local app data in device backups that you control.

Advertising, Analytics, and Tracking

Pegloom does not contain advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking technology, or third-party login services. The game does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you across apps or websites.

Network Access and Third-Party Services

The current version of Pegloom is designed to work offline and does not transmit gameplay information to external servers. No third-party service receives information from the game.

Sound and Haptic Feedback

Sound and haptic feedback are generated by the app and device. Pegloom does not access or record microphone input.

Children's Privacy

Because Pegloom does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Game data remains on your device until it is reset by the operating system or the app is removed. Restored device backups may restore local app data.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the game's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.
